Frequently Asked Questions

What is EdlioX?

EdlioX is a platform that connects educators and students worldwide, allowing them to share and access educational resources, engage in discussions, and monetize their content.

How do I get started?

Simply create an account, complete your profile, and you can start uploading resources or participating in discussions. You can also browse existing resources and join discussions without creating an account.

Is it free to use?

Yes, basic features are free. You can browse resources, participate in discussions, and upload content without any cost. Premium features and monetization options are available for content creators.

What types of resources can I upload?

You can upload various educational materials including documents, presentations, videos, and interactive content. All content must be original or properly attributed.

What are the file size limits?

Maximum file size is 100MB per upload. For larger files, consider splitting them into smaller parts or using external storage with links.

How do I monetize my content?

To monetize your content, you need to meet certain quality criteria and apply through our monetization program. Approved content can be sold to users.

How do discussions work?

Discussions are topic-based conversations where users can ask questions, share insights, and help each other. You can start a new discussion or participate in existing ones.

Are there discussion guidelines?

Yes, we have community guidelines to ensure respectful and productive discussions. Users should maintain professional conduct and avoid inappropriate content.

How do I report inappropriate content?

You can report inappropriate content using the report button on any resource or discussion. Our moderation team will review and take appropriate action.

How do I reset my password?

Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email address.

How is my data protected?

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your personal information is never shared with third parties without your consent.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, you can delete your account through your profile settings. Note that this action cannot be undone.
